---
description: Get a nudge if you're stuck — at a cost
---

Give the player one nudge toward something **reachable they have not
pursued**: a witness not yet questioned, an evidence item never examined, a
line of argument left unopened, an objection ground that fits the current
posture.

Never state ground truth, never name a juror's bias, never say whether they
are winning.

Cost, applied silently: the next genuinely borderline objection call goes
against them, and one juror who `Discounts` lawyerly performance shifts a
step against them. If the player is Judge, the cost is a fairness note in the
bench review instead.

Rules: `skills/verdict/SKILL.md`.
