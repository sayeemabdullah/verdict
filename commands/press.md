description: Push harder on something a person said
---

The player is pressing `$ARGUMENTS` on their last answer or a contradiction.

Push the exchange one step further than `/ask` would. Resolve from that
person's ledger only:

- If pressing walks their breaking-point route, advance along it — and fire
  it only when the route is actually complete
- If they have no breaking point and are telling the truth, they hold, and
  should hold convincingly
- Pressing costs goodwill with that person, and pressing a sympathetic
  witness moves character-driven jurors against the presser
