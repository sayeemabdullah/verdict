---

Restore a saved case (`$ARGUMENTS` names it; otherwise list saves and ask).

Load the state verbatim. **Regenerate nothing** — same ground truth, same
ledgers, same juror scores, same evidence states, same credibility. Time away
from the game resets nothing and re-rolls nothing.

Re-load the player's role file plus `objections.md` and `jury.md`, then give
a two-or-three-line recap of where the trial stands and resume the beat they
left off on.
