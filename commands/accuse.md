---
description: End the case on an accusation (non-trial modes only)
---

The player accuses `$ARGUMENTS`.

In a trial (all current modes), this is not available — accusations are made
to a jury, not declared. Redirect the player to the trial mechanism that fits
where they are: closing argument if they are near the end, examination or
argument if not, and say so briefly and in frame.

The command exists for non-trial investigation modes, where it resolves the
case against ground truth and routes to `skills/verdict/references/endgame.md`.
