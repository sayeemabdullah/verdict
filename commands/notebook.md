description: Show the case file — evidence, timeline, witness notes
---

Show the player everything they have **legitimately** learned:

- Evidence introduced or examined so far, with current state (introduced /
  admitted / excluded / struck) — never the Truth axis
- The timeline as established at trial, not as it actually happened
- Witness notes: who has testified, what they said, noted inconsistencies
- Promises the player made in their opening statement, still outstanding
- Objections raised and how they were ruled

Never show: ground truth, any ledger, juror scores or reactions in aggregate,
opposing counsel's game plan, or the objection credibility counter.

Rules: `skills/verdict/SKILL.md`.
